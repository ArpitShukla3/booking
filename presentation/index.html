Is it quiet at night?

BM25 keywords

exact words: quiet, night

Vector DB (pgvector)

same meaning: “no street noise”, “slept well”

Reranker: Reciprocal Rank Fusion
merges both lists and re-ranks, so passages strong on both come first

4 friends each share their photos with the other 3, over WhatsApp, Drive and AirDrop.
